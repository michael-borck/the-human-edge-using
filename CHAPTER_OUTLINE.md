## 1. Why this book, and why separate from CND

*Conversation, Not Delegation* is the general method for thinking with AI, any discipline, broadest audience. This book is the workplace application: short, practical, anchored in tasks professionals already do. Shared foundations (RTCF, VET, Gell-Mann amnesia) get one working chapter here and a pointer to the deeper treatment there.

New material that exists nowhere else in the catalogue: the Trust Tool as the organising spine (it is an appendix in *Prove, Don't Persuade*), the Opportunity Finder with the 30-day gate, Decide Like Me, Write Like Me as a standing brief, the RetailFlow three-claims case, and Australian practice and evidence (six essential practices, Privacy Act APP 1.7 to 1.9, DEWR and Jobs and Skills Australia findings).

Framing hook: vibe professionalism. Used in the Preface and Chapter 1 only. The durable title language stays "judgement", in lockstep with the Curtin catalogue.

## 2. Audience

Professionals in any role who use or are about to use AI at work, including team leaders. No technical background. Executives choosing investments are served by Leading, not here; the Preface routes them.

## 4. Design decisions

1. Four parts mirror the four course sessions, one tool and one question each. A participant can find the book chapter for any moment of the day.
2. Exercises stay in Appendix C as well as chapter callouts. Course participants use the workbook online; book readers need them in print.
3. Everything that dates fast (menus, model names, jargon, Northbridge injection demo) sits in Appendix E, so reprints touch one appendix.
4. Australian material is boxed into Appendix D and Chapter 11 evidence. The four tools stay jurisdiction-free.
5. KDP positioning: short practical book. Target 35,000 to 45,000 words after editorial pass. Seed is close; expansion should come from worked examples, not new frameworks.

## 5. What would make it fail

- Reading as CND retitled. Guard: workplace tasks and cases carry every chapter; shared methods stay summary-length.
- Reading as course slides with glue. Guard: narrative chapters with named cases; speaker-note content expanded, slide text never pasted raw.
- The generic title. Guard: subtitle does the positioning; series branding matches Curtin exactly.
